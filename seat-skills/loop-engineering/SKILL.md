# Run a bounded implementation loop

Work through observe, hypothesize, make one scoped change, test, and inspect evidence. Limit retries to the budget in the brief; preserve the failing output. Repair the cause, not just the test. Stop when scope or permissions are insufficient. Do not retry by changing vendor, model, effort, panel membership or rules; those are dispatcher decisions. Long-running work remains a single leaf seat, not a manager.
