The panel

Different vendors. Different failure modes.

MAGI is not one model pretending to be a committee. Melchior, Balthasar, and Casper are vendor-separated seats. Grok sits above them only as classifier, dispatcher, evidence recorder, and mechanical arbiter.

Melchior / OpenAI

Technical depth, implementation, debugging, and adversarial review. The OpenAI ladder scales from Luna through Terra and Sol to Astra for extreme or explicitly escalated work.

Balthasar / Anthropic

Judgment, conservative review, planning, safety, and long-running agentic implementation. Sonnet, Fable, and Opus are selected by role rather than treated as one linear ladder.

Casper / Google

Context, breadth, synthesis, and an intentionally decorrelated third perspective. agy results must carry exact observed-model proof so silent downgrade cannot masquerade as the requested seat.

Routing

The dispatch matrix is executable policy.

Grok classifies the unit and proposes a route. The validator allows only role, model, effort, vendor, escalation, and availability combinations admitted by the machine-readable matrix.

Control plane

Rules become gates, not suggestions.

MAGI CLI validates the whole plan before spend, then re-validates every seat at the actual launch boundary. A deterministic failure cannot be waived by Grok or by a seat.

1. ClassifyGrok labels the unit and proposes a semantic role and legal route.
2. Validate planDistribution, model, effort, probe, escalation, and review rules.
3. Bind seatPermissions, minimal staged skills, leaf-seat contract.
4. ExecuteVendor CLI runs with role-scoped permissions.
5. ProveIDs, usage, capture, model identity evidence, and hashes.
6. TallyStructured receipts drive activation and panel result.
Standing design

What MAGI refuses to trust.

01
No self-gradingA vendor cannot review or verify the artifact it authored.
02
No arbiter seatGrok classifies and dispatches but never supplies the substantive seat result or vote.
03
No invented identity evidenceObserved model identity is recorded only when the vendor path exposes it. Claude's current text-mode path is explicitly labeled requested-only.
04
No hidden skill sprawlSeats receive a staged allow-listed skill pack matched to role and task class. Bridges and orchestration remain arbiter-only.
05
No fake distribution mathThe 60% implementation floor starts at two implement units; plan, research, and review panels never fabricate implementation rows.
06
No prose-only complianceRules packs, seat profiles, skill manifests, receipts, telemetry, and matrix versions are hashed or schema-checked.
