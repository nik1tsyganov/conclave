The panel

Different vendors. Different failure modes.

MAGI is not one model pretending to be a committee. Melchior, Balthasar, and Casper are vendor-separated seats. Grok sits above them only as dispatcher and mechanical arbiter.

Melchior / OpenAI

Technical depth, implementation, debugging, and adversarial review. The OpenAI ladder scales from Luna through Terra and Sol to Astra for extreme work.

Balthasar / Anthropic

Judgment, conservative review, long-running agentic implementation, and safety. Sonnet, Fable, and Opus are selected by role rather than treated as one linear ladder.

Casper / Google

Context, breadth, synthesis, and an intentionally decorrelated third perspective. Every agy route requires exact observed-model proof to prevent silent substitution.

Routing

The dispatch matrix is executable policy.

Grok classifies the unit and proposes a route. The validator allows only model, effort, vendor, and role combinations admitted by the machine-readable matrix.

Control plane

Rules become gates, not suggestions.

MAGI CLI validates the whole plan before spend, then re-validates every seat at the actual launch boundary. A deterministic failure cannot be waived by Grok or by a seat.

1. ClassifyGrok labels the unit and proposes routes.
2. Validate planDistribution, model, effort, probe, and review rules.
3. Bind seatRole, permissions, minimal skills, leaf-seat contract.
4. ExecuteVendor CLI runs with scoped permissions.
5. ProveObserved model, IDs, usage, capture, hashes.
6. TallyStructured receipts drive activation and panel result.
Standing design

What MAGI refuses to trust.

01
No self-gradingA vendor cannot review or verify the artifact it authored.
02
No arbiter implementationGrok may classify and dispatch, but never writes product work or casts a vote.
03
No claimed model identityVendor-native proof must show what actually ran.
04
No hidden skill sprawlSeats receive a staged allow-listed skill pack matched to vendor, role, and task class.
05
No silent concentrationDistribution rules prevent the host vendor from quietly implementing everything when qualified alternatives exist.
06
No prose-only complianceRules packs, seat profiles, receipts, telemetry, and matrix versions are hashed or schema-checked.
